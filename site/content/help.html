The help inside the program knows more than this site

Press F1 in TrestleBoard and ask in your own words — how do I put a photo on the page, birthdays, send it out. Every answer has a Take me there button that runs the command for you, and because it is standing in your newsletter it can also tell you why something is not available right now, in a sentence.

There is also a short tour the first time you open the program — five screens on how a month goes. You can see it again at any time from Help → Show me around.
